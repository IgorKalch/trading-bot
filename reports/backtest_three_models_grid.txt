--- D. THE COST STRUCTURE IS THE REAL LESSON ---
Average cost per trade, in R, by model:
  orb (stop = full opening range, ~75 pts)        0.067R
  sweep M5 (stop beyond the sweep, ~40 pts)       0.113-0.118R
  retest / sweep M1 (stop ~25-30 pts)             0.157-0.180R

Every tightening of the stop that makes a model look attractive on paper
multiplies what the spread takes. A model on 27-point stops must find more
than 0.16R of edge per trade before it breaks even - and 0.16R is roughly the
size of the largest raw effect anything here produced, with an error bar
wider than the effect.

--- E. WHAT THIS ROUND ADDS TO THE VERDICT ---
Continuation fails (ORB, retest). Fade fails too (sweep), and fails harder:
-0.11R to -0.28R across every exit. The imbalance filter moves cells by a few
hundredths of an R in both directions, which is what a filter does when there
is nothing to filter.

Three model families, nine exit schemes, three data sources, four years.
The opening range of the DAX does not carry a tradeable edge in any of the
forms tested - as a breakout, as a pullback entry, or as a fade.
