honest source of that is more trades. Three candidates were tested on NQ.

The legs are genuinely uncorrelated - daily correlation +0.05 and -0.08 - so
diversification works exactly as theory says it should. It does not help,
because the added legs have no edge. retest + sweep raises monthly R from
1.16 to 1.43 and raises the drawdown from 10.3R to 35.0R. Return per unit of
drawdown falls from 0.113 to 0.041.

--- E. CONCLUSION ---
There is no second session, no better range length, and no second model. The
configuration already in hand - retest, M1, 09:30 New York, 5-minute opening
range, pullback window 6, fixed 1R, FVG + MA89 - is the best version of the
only thing in this project that works.

It makes 1.16R a month against a 10.3R drawdown. That is 0.58% a month at
0.5% risk, and roughly 12 to 17 months to a 10% challenge target at a risk
level a daily loss limit permits. Nothing tested changes that, and the search
for something that does is now exhausted along every axis this project has.
