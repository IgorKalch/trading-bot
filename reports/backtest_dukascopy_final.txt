--- D. THE EXACT ANATOMY OF THE FALSE POSITIVE ---
The window the brokers could serve was 2026-05-18 .. 2026-08-28, 75 sessions.

  same window, FTMO data,      maxPB 3, 24 trades:   PF 1.29   +0.130R
  same window, FTMO data,      maxPB 6, 41 trades:   PF 1.08   +0.039R
  same window, Dukascopy data, maxPB 6, 38 trades:   PF 0.61   -0.251R

The same model, over the same 75 sessions, on two feeds of the same market,
gives +0.039R and -0.251R. The sample was never large enough to have a sign,
let alone a size. The 95% interval computed at the time, -0.290R .. +0.551R,
said exactly this in advance, and the wider data has now confirmed it.

--- E. VERDICT ---
Both candidates are closed:
  ORB           - 18 months, two brokers, every exit, 21,672 filter
                  combinations, clean holdout. No edge.
  Retest on M1  - 943 sessions, 445 trades, every year negative, and no edge
                  even at zero cost. Significantly losing.

Nothing in this project is profitable, and this is now a measurement rather
than an absence of evidence. The infrastructure did its job: it produced two
false positives on small samples and then killed both when given enough data.
