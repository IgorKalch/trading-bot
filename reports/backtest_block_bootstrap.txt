===== BLOCK BOOTSTRAP: DOES CLUSTERING INFLATE THE EDGE? =====

retest / NQ M1 / 09:30 NY / OR 5 min / pullback 6 / fixed 1R / FVG + MA89
349 trades across 44 months, expectancy +0.1456R

  method                     95% interval on expectancy   resamples below zero
  trade-level (independent)     +0.0416 .. +0.2493              0.29%
  block by month                +0.0514 .. +0.2397              0.14%
  block by quarter (15 blocks)  +0.0585 .. +0.2327              0.03%

Block resampling normally WIDENS the interval, because it stops the bootstrap
from pretending clustered outcomes are independent draws. Here it narrows it
slightly, which means monthly and quarterly variance is close to what
independence predicts: there is no hidden clustering propping the estimate up.

Monthly R: mean +1.16R, median +0.98R, worst -5.07R, best +5.97R,
27 of 44 months positive (61%).

This is the strongest statistical support the result has had. It does not
address the two things bootstrapping cannot: that NDXUSD is a CFD proxy for the
NQ future, and that one-tick slippage is assumed rather than measured.
