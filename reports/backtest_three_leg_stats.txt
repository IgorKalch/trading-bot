============================ WHAT TO READ FROM IT ============================

1. THE COST LEVEL DECIDES WHICH LEGS EXIST. At futures cost all three clear
   zero. At prop CFD cost the IB confluence leg does NOT (lower bound -0.0112)
   and the retest leg only barely does (+0.0003). Only drive is comfortable
   (+0.0018 on a much larger sample, and it was the leg most improved by the
   3.0R change). On a CFD account this is closer to a one-and-a-half leg
   portfolio than a three-leg one.

2. DRIVE IS NOW THE LARGEST LEG BY EVERY MEASURE - 906 trades, +2.459 R/mo
   alone, MAR 4.35 - and that is a change from a week ago, caused entirely by
   the target sweep. It is also the leg with the WORST out-of-sample story
   (+0.141 in-sample against +0.088 out), which is the reason its 3.0R target
   was adopted for the drawdown and not for the return.

3. THE COMBINATION STILL BEATS ITS PARTS, but less impressively than before,
   because confluence and drive now correlate +0.44 - they share the session
   open structure. Retest against drive is +0.11 and retest against confluence
   +0.03, so the retest leg is doing most of the diversification work despite
   being the smallest.
   Note 1+3 alone: MAR 4.96 with R/mo +1.810, against 5.58 and +1.616 for all
   three. The third leg buys drawdown, not return - the same conclusion the
   scaling work reached, now with different legs.

4. TRADE FREQUENCY IS HIGH: 45 trades a month across the portfolio, roughly two
   per session. That is a very different operational load from the retest leg
   alone at 7 a month, and it matters for a manual trader - drive and confluence
   both take something almost every session.

5. THE HONEST MONEY ANSWER. At 1% total risk and futures cost: +20.7% a year
   with a 3.5% maximum drawdown, measured. At 2% total: +44.3% with 7.0%. At
   prop CFD cost the same rows read +15.4%/4.8% and +31.9%/9.7%.
   A 2% total risk on a CFD account puts the historical maximum drawdown at
   9.7% against a 10% limit, which is too close: a historical maximum is a
   floor on the future one, not a ceiling. 1.0-1.5% total is the defensible
   range, giving +15-24% a year at a 5-7% drawdown.

6. WORST MONTH is -3.5R combined at futures cost, against -5.1, -5.5 and -6.8
   for the legs alone. At 1% total risk that is a 3.5% month. Four of ten
   months are still losers on every leg.

CAVEATS THAT DO NOT SHRINK: NQ only - none of these models is positive on the
other six index markets. NDXUSD is a CFD proxy, not the NQ future. Slippage is
assumed at one tick and has never been measured on a live account. And drive's
3.0R target was adopted on a drawdown improvement whose paired bootstrap gives
a 21.5% chance the drawdown is actually worse.
