===== DEEPER HISTORY: THE HOLDOUT TEST THAT CLOSES ORB =====

After raising 'Max bars in chart' in both terminals:
  M5   69,597 -> 100,136 bars (FundingPips) / 100,762 (FTMO)  = ~18 months
  M1  100,000 -> 100,000 bars on BOTH, unchanged

The M1 limit is server-side, not a terminal setting: copy_rates_range for
2024 or early 2025 returns a single bar on both brokers. Neither will serve
more than 100,000 M1 bars, so the retest model cannot be tested any further
on data from these two firms.

What the deeper M5 buys is more valuable: 2025-03-16 .. 2025-08-21 was never
touched by any test, sweep or parameter choice in this project. It is a true
holdout, and every number below for it is a first look.

On FundingPips the two settings that looked best on the tuning period -- 0.20
and 0.30, at +0.088R and +0.131R -- are the two WORST on data they never saw,
at -0.092R and -0.132R. The ranking does not merely weaken, it inverts. That is
the textbook signature of a parameter fitted to noise.

The two brokers also disagree about which value is best on the same holdout
(FundingPips 0.15, FTMO 0.20), which is the second tell.

And the baseline itself tells the real story: frac 0.00 is +0.009R and +0.028R
on the holdout against -0.099R and -0.104R on the tuning period. The holdout
was simply a friendlier five months for opening-range breakouts. What looked
like a filter finding was period, not edge.

--- C. VERDICT ON ORB ---
Closed. The model was tested on two brokers, over eighteen months, across
every exit scheme and 21,672 filter combinations, and given a clean holdout at
the end. It has no edge to find. Further parameter work on it is not research,
it is decoration.

The retest model on M1 remains the only live candidate, and it is now blocked
on data: 24-41 trades is all these brokers can supply, and its 95% confidence
interval (-0.290R .. +0.551R) contains zero. Answering it needs M1 history
from outside these two firms.
