===== SECOND BROKER: FTMO-Demo (GER40.cash) =====
Purpose: run the same models against a different broker's quotes for the same
market and period, to separate 'the model fails' from 'this feed is odd'.

FundingPips  GER40       M5 69,597 bars  258 sessions  contract 25.0  1pt = 28.95 USD/lot
FTMO-Demo    GER40.cash  M5 69,454 bars  257 sessions  contract  1.0  1pt =  1.16 USD/lot
Both feeds put the busiest bar of every session at 09:00 Europe/Berlin, so
server_timezone eet_us_dst is correct for both.

NOTE ON COSTS: spread 2.0 + slippage 1.0 is applied to BOTH. The FTMO spread
could not be measured (weekend, no live tick) and is a placeholder carried over
from FundingPips. The comparison between the two columns is therefore fair;
the absolute level of either is not settled until both are measured live.

Both feeds agree on the DIRECTION: demanding a decisive break improves the
baseline. They disagree on the SIZE by a factor of about seven at frac 0.40
(+0.184R vs +0.026R), and on FTMO the out-of-sample sign flips negative.
On FundingPips this filter looked like the first real finding of the project.
On a parallel feed of the same market over the same days it is break-even.
The effect is inside the noise that merely changing data vendor produces.

--- C. RETEST MODEL ON M1: THE ONE THAT SURVIVES THE SWAP ---
Same 75 sessions (2026-05-18..08-28), maxPB 6, fixed 1R:
  costs                        | FundingPips  n     PF     expR | FTMO  n     PF     expR
  real (spread 2.0 + slip 1)  |         38   0.92   -0.041 |    41   1.06   +0.033
  spread 1.0 + slip 0.5       |         38   0.96   -0.020 |    41   1.22   +0.101
  zero costs                  |         38   1.38   +0.158 |    41   1.55   +0.217

  Pullback window, real costs:   FundingPips        FTMO
    maxPB  3    22 trades  PF 0.92  -0.043     24 trades  PF 1.27  +0.124
    maxPB  6    38 trades  PF 0.92  -0.041     41 trades  PF 1.06  +0.033
    maxPB 10    55 trades  PF 0.72  -0.169     54 trades  PF 0.74  -0.155
    maxPB 15    64 trades  PF 0.82  -0.101     62 trades  PF 0.77  -0.138

Both feeds agree that the raw signal has an edge (PF 1.38 / 1.55 at zero cost)
and that a short pullback window is what carries it -- 3 to 6 bars works, 10 to
15 does not, which matches the source's 'ideally inside three minutes'.
On FTMO it stays positive after real costs; on FundingPips it does not.

--- D. WHAT THE TWO BROKERS DO AND DO NOT PROVE ---
These are NOT two independent samples. Same index, same days, same trades --
only the quotes differ. Agreement rules out an artefact of one feed. It says
nothing about another time period, and 22-41 trades remains far too few.
What section B shows is how much a change of vendor alone can move a measured
effect: enough to turn a 'first real finding' into break-even.
