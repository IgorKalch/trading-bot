===== THE "INDICES VLAD GAP" MATERIAL, TESTED =====

Source: three new videos in grafik-creen (a 1h53m strategy course plus two
backtest sessions), 2560x1440 screen recordings with no subtitle track. Frames
were extracted with ffmpeg and read directly; the course shows its written
algorithm as a Notion page, which is where the rules below come from.

--- WHAT THE MATERIAL ACTUALLY IS ---
An ICT / smart-money framework, stated by the author as five ordered elements:
time, liquidity, POI (order block / rejection block / inefficiency: FVG, VI,
inversion FVG, BPR), manipulation as the origin or continuation of order flow,
and target. The operative sentences are about reading a narrative - "before the
NYSE open I define the main narrative of price action so that I understand which
liquidity the manipulation after 16:30 may be aimed at" - and about the MMXM
market model, breaker plus FVG on m15-m5, and respecting zones of interest.

That is discretionary by construction. It is the same class of material as the
YouTube sources reviewed earlier: a way of seeing, not a decision rule.

--- WHAT WAS MECHANICALLY EXTRACTABLE AND NEW ---
Two things, and they are genuinely new to this project:

1. The level set. The author sweeps PDH/PDL and PWH/PWL - the previous session's
   and previous week's extremes - as "the key liquidity raid inside the day that
   seeds a new order flow". Every sweep test here so far used the OVERNIGHT
   range instead.
2. The NY Midnight / true day open as a magnet. Not tested, see below.

Implemented as sweep.reference = overnight | prev_day | prev_week, leaving the
state machine (sweep the level, close back inside, fade with the stop beyond the
sweep extreme) untouched, so only the level changes.

Eighteen cells across three markets and two timeframes. Not one has a 95%
interval above zero. The best, NQ prev_day on M5 at +0.034R, reads +0.071
in-sample and -0.019 out-of-sample - the sign flips, which is the pattern this
project has now seen four times and learned to distrust.

PDH/PDL does beat the overnight range on NQ (+0.034R against -0.040R) and halves
the drawdown, 43.7R to 17.6R. That is a better level set. It is not an edge.

--- NOT TESTED ---
The NY Midnight / true day open as a magnet. It is mechanically definable - the
price at 00:00 New York - but it is a target rule rather than an entry rule, and
this project has no model whose exit it would improve. Worth revisiting only if
an entry with an edge ever needs a better target.

--- WHERE THIS LEAVES THINGS ---
The material adds a framework and a better level set, and no edge. The NQ retest
model at +0.146R remains the only thing measured in this project that beats its
own error bar.
