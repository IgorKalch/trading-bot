pullback window 6, futures-like costs 0.25 + 0.25.

byte-identical numbers. That was not a finding - trend_ma, OR width,
skip_weekdays and min_or_rvol were implemented in orb.py only, so on the
retest model they did nothing at all. They are now wired into retest.py and
the numbers below are real.

--- D. AGAINST A 10% PROP LIMIT ---
  risk 0.25%:  max DD 2.6%   safe    ~35 months to a 10% target

The honest reading: about 0.6% a month at a risk level that survives the
account rule. Thirty-nine of every hundred months are negative and the worst
historical month is -2.5%.

--- E. WHAT WOULD MAKE THIS WRONG ---
1. Eighteen filter combinations were tested here. FVG and MA89 both came with
   a prior - the imbalance from the smart-money material, the moving average
   from Hougaard - and both improve in-sample AND out-of-sample AND trim20,
   which is the pattern a real effect makes. It is still selection.
2. 349 trades over 3.66 years. The monthly figures rest on 44 months.
3. Slippage of one tick is assumed, never measured. On a 35-point stop each
   extra tick costs 0.7% of R - roughly 0.007R, so it would take several ticks
   of unmodelled slippage to erase +0.146R. Not fragile, but not verified.
4. NDXUSD is a CFD proxy for the NQ future.
