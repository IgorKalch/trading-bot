===== ACROSS INSTRUMENTS: THE FIRST POSITIVE RESULT =====

Data: Dukascopy M1, 2023-01-02 .. 2026-08-28, ~940-950 sessions each.
  DEUIDXEUR (DAX)  1,225,546 bars   session 09:00-17:30 Europe/Berlin
  SPXUSD    (ES)   1,231,758 bars   session 09:30-16:00 America/New_York
  NDXUSD    (NQ)   1,249,440 bars   session 09:30-16:00 America/New_York

Measured CFD spreads in the entry window: DAX 1.51, ES 0.50, NQ 1.34 points.
Futures-equivalent costs used below: one tick of spread plus one tick of
slippage - 0.5+0.5 for FDAX, 0.25+0.25 for ES and NQ.

--- A. THE SAME MODEL ON THREE INSTRUMENTS ---
retest, pullback window 6, fixed 1R, futures-like costs:

  instrument     n      WR     PF     expR    +-SE   mean 1R   cost/R
  DAX / FDAX   445   49.0%   0.92   -0.044   0.049     22.2     4.5%
  ES           477   45.5%   0.80   -0.114   0.047      8.2     6.1%
  NQ           428   55.1%   1.21   +0.096   0.049     35.1     1.4%

Expectancy is monotone in cost-per-R. NQ swings 35 points on a 5-minute
pullback while its tick is 0.25, so a round trip costs 1.4% of the risk. ES
moves 8 points on the same setup and pays 6.1%. That ordering is the whole
story of the last four months of this project, in one table.

It is not only cost: NQ's win rate is 55.1% against 49.0% and 45.5%, so the
signal itself is better there too, and the cheap ticks let it survive.

--- C. THE TWO NQ VARIANTS, AND WHY THE BORING ONE IS BETTER ---

  retest pb6, trailing 1.0/1.0:  n=428  WR 28.5%  PF 1.44  +0.222R +-0.088
  retest pb6, fixed 1R:          n=428  WR 55.1%  PF 1.21  +0.096R +-0.049

The trailing version looks twice as good and is far more fragile. Removing
its best trades:
    top  5 removed  +0.222R -> +0.129R
    top 10 removed          -> +0.061R
    top 20 removed          -> -0.051R
Twenty trades out of 428 carry the entire result. The fixed-1R version holds:
    top  5 removed  +0.096R -> +0.085R
    top 20 removed          -> +0.052R
    top 40 removed          -> +0.003R

--- D. EVERY TEST RUN AGAINST THE FIXED-1R RESULT ---
  95% interval            +0.001 .. +0.191   (barely clears zero)
  in-sample / out-of-sample   +0.110 -> +0.076   (split at 2025-03-01)
  2023 +0.196   2024 +0.045   2025 +0.011   2026 +0.132   (all positive)
  long +0.145 (n=224)   short +0.042 (n=204)   (not a bull-market artefact)
  null model              -0.028R, sd 0.027 across 12 seeds
  real vs null            4.6 standard deviations above

The null model matters most. It fires a random entry at a random time on a
random side, with the SAME stop sizes and the SAME exit machinery, on the same
bars. It returns -0.028R. So the edge is in the entry, not in the trailing
exit and not in NQ's 167% rise over the period.

--- E. WHAT IT WOULD PAY, AND WHAT IT WOULD COST ---
  0.45 trades per session, 428 trades over 952 sessions
  fixed 1R:      +41.1R total, max drawdown 12.6R
     at 1.0% risk: +41% over 3.7 years, 12.6% peak drawdown
     at 0.5% risk: +21% over 3.7 years,  6.3% peak drawdown

At 1% risk the drawdown breaches a 10% prop limit. At 0.5% it fits, and the
return falls to about 5.5% a year - real, but not a fast route to anything.

--- F. WHAT WOULD STILL KILL THIS ---
1. MULTIPLE TESTING. 594 cells were examined. At 95% confidence roughly 30
   false positives are expected by chance across that many tests; 7 appeared,
   all in one family. The confirmatory tests in section D are the real
   evidence, not the interval.
2. The interval's lower bound is +0.001. One bad quarter moves it below zero.
3. NDXUSD is Dukascopy's CFD, a proxy for the NQ future. Real NQ futures data
   and real fills have not been tested.
4. Slippage of one tick is an assumption. On a 35-point stop each extra tick
   costs 0.7% of R, so it is not fatal, but it is not measured either.
