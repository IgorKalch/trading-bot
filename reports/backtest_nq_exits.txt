--- THE COLUMN THAT DECIDES IT: trim20 ---
trim20 is expectancy after deleting the 20 best trades of 428.

  EVERY trailing variant goes NEGATIVE: -0.044 to -0.184.
  EVERY fixed variant stays POSITIVE:   +0.002 to +0.062.

Trailing reads better on the headline number - up to +0.269R against +0.117R -
and it is entirely carried by a handful of runners. Delete twenty trades out
of four hundred and it is a losing strategy. The fixed exits keep their edge
when the same twenty are deleted, which is the property that matters for
something you would actually run.
