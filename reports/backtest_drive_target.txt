--- WHY THIS IS NOT THE "LET WINNERS RUN" ILLUSION ---
Twenty-two trailing and give-back exit variants have been measured in this
project across two legs, and every single one went NEGATIVE on trim20 - delete
the twenty best trades and the edge was gone, because it lived in a handful of
runners. This does the opposite:

  trim20:   2.0R +0.0460   2.5R +0.0561   3.0R +0.0545   3.5R +0.0527
            no cap -0.0451

Removing the target ENTIRELY is decisively worse on every column - drawdown
19.0R, trim20 negative. So the effect is an INTERIOR PLATEAU, not an
unbounded-upside artefact, and 2.5/3.0/3.5 sit flat at drawdown 7.2/7.3/6.8.
3.0R is chosen as the middle of that plateau, not as the best cell; 3.5R is
nominally better on expectancy and the difference is noise.

===== WHERE I CORRECT THE RESEARCH AGENT =====

The synthesis reported the paired quarterly bootstrap of the drawdown change as
"median -4.1R, 95% [-15.4, +1.0], P(3.0R worse) = 5.4%". My own run, 3000 draws,
both arms resampled on the SAME quarters:

    median -3.31R   95% [-17.08, +6.00]   P(3.0R has the WORSE drawdown) = 21.5%

That is a materially weaker result than reported, and the confidence interval
crosses zero by a wide margin. The drawdown improvement is DIRECTIONALLY
supported and NOT statistically established: roughly one chance in five that
3.0R actually has the worse drawdown. Do not quote the 5.4%.

Everything else in the synthesis replicated: the plateau, the no-cap control,
the trim20 direction, the CFD-cost crossover, both-halves drawdown, 2023
turning positive, and the paired per-trade test.

===== THE HONEST HAZARDS =====
1. THE EXPECTANCY GAIN IS NOT SIGNIFICANT. Paired on the same days,
   +0.0303R with t = 1.57, and the outcome changes on only 22% of trades.
2. OUT-OF-SAMPLE EXPECTANCY FALLS: +0.1206 at 2.0R against +0.0885 at 3.0R.
   The IS/OOS pattern is reversed from the drawdown. Adopt this for the
   drawdown and budget NOTHING for the return.
3. MORE WEIGHT ON THE CLOSE PRINT. Target hits fall from 22% of trades to 11%,
   and trades resolving at the 15:55 close rise from 31% to 39%. The equity
   curve leans harder on one print per day, and on whatever difference exists
   between a CFD close and a futures settlement.
4. WIN RATE FALLS 44.9% to 42.8%. Psychologically this is a harder model to
   trade by hand, on top of an already low win rate.
5. Six targets plus a control were compared. The plateau and the control are
   what make this more than a best-cell pick, not the individual numbers.

===== THE LESSON, WHICH IS ABOUT METHOD =====
tools/filters.py is a boolean mask over a fixed vector of trade outcomes. It
cannot express any rule that MUTATES an outcome rather than deleting a trade.
Every proposal in the last two sessions was therefore shaped as an entry gate -
twenty of them, all killed with the same signature, %trades removed equal to
%losses removed - while the exit surface of the newest and largest leg sat
unswept because the harness could not ask about it.

The twenty kills are one finding, not twenty: the drive model's losers are not
identifiable at 10:00 from any session statistic - profile, volatility, volume,
width or location. Stop testing members of that class. The next harness needs to
accept an outcome-mutating rule and report drawdown, trim20 and a paired
bootstrap against a named benchmark.

Also recorded, from the search: the 76.4% open-type win rate this project has
been quoting as the claim it refuted has NO TRACEABLE SOURCE across twelve
search angles. Our closed findings should be stated against the measured
baselines instead - a clean drive-direction day runs at 49.3% on NQ and 43.1%
on DAX.
