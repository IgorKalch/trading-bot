===== THE HORIZON HYPOTHESIS, TESTED =====

The literature search named holding horizon as the one dimension never
searched, on the argument that cost/R is cost divided by stop distance, stop
distance grows with holding time, and cost does not. Built the universe and
the backtester and tested it.

--- WHAT WAS BUILT ---
23 markets of DAILY bars from Dukascopy, five asset classes, up to 18 years:
  equity  NQ ES YM DAX SX5E FTSE NKY        (2012-2026)
  fx      EURUSD GBPUSD USDJPY AUDUSD USDCAD USDCHF   (2008-2026)
  metals  XAUUSD XAGUSD COPPER
  energy  BRENT WTI NATGAS
  bonds   BUND GILT USTB     crypto  BTCUSD

tools/daily.py - a separate backtester, because the intraday engine flattens
at flat_time by construction and cannot hold a position overnight.

--- TWO BUGS FOUND WHILE BUILDING IT, BOTH INFLATING RESULTS ---
1. R was computed against the TRAILED stop rather than the initial one, which
   shrinks the denominator on winners. First run reported +0.55R per trade on
   NQ and PF 2.17. After the fix: -0.003R and PF 0.99.
2. Entry fired on the persistent signal STATE, so after a stop-out the system
   re-entered the same direction on the very next bar. That manufactures
   whipsaw. Fixed to enter only on a fresh signal transition.
   Trade count fell from 4,601 to 2,233 on donchian-20.

--- THE HYPOTHESIS IS HALF RIGHT ---
It delivers exactly what was predicted on cost and on per-trade edge:

  system            trades   /mo     WR    PF     expR
  donchian 200 *       241   1.4  19.5%  1.56   +0.446
  donchian 300 *       173   1.0  16.8%  1.55   +0.459
  macross 300 *        344   2.0  20.1%  1.41   +0.320
  donchian 200        250   1.4  38.8%  1.41   +0.168
  tsmom 20           9127  51.9  33.3%  0.99   -0.002
  macross 20         5855  33.3  34.7%  0.99   -0.004
  (* = no trailing stop, exit on signal flip only)

+0.446R per trade is THREE TIMES the intraday NQ edge of +0.146R. Long-horizon
trend following on a diversified universe is real, and costs are irrelevant to
it - running the same test at zero cost moves donchian 200 from +0.263R to
+0.273R. Short lookbacks are all dead: tsmom and macross under 100 days sit at
PF 0.99 across thousands of trades.

--- AND IT FAILS ON THE ONLY METRIC THAT MATTERS HERE ---
Both systems scaled so simultaneous risk equals 1R:

  system                            months   R/month     DD    annual MAR   +months
  intraday NQ retest                    44   +1.155    8.32R      1.67        61%
  daily trend, 23 markets              116   +0.040    2.35R      0.21        26%

Monthly correlation between them: -0.19. They genuinely diversify - the trend
leg is simply too weak to be worth adding, and every blend lands between the
two, closer to the weaker one.

Trade frequency is why. donchian 200 produces 1.4 trades a month across
TWENTY-THREE markets. At +0.446R that is 0.6R a month of gross edge before
the drawdown clustering that comes from holding correlated positions.

--- WHAT THE TARGET ACTUALLY ASKS FOR ---
  3R/month at a 10R drawdown = monthly MAR 0.3 = ANNUAL MAR 3.6
  6R/month at a 10R drawdown = monthly MAR 0.6 = ANNUAL MAR 7.2

  measured here, intraday NQ:                 annual MAR 1.35 - 1.67
  measured here, daily trend on 23 markets:   annual MAR 0.21
  professional CTAs, documented:              annual MAR 0.30 - 0.70
  best honest number in the literature file:  annual MAR 1.35

The target is between 2.7x and 5.3x the best result this project has produced,
and roughly 5x to 10x what the trend-following industry achieves. Nothing in
the 64 candidates the literature search surfaced comes close either.

--- CONCLUSION ---
The most profitable system that can be defended remains the intraday NQ retest
at annual MAR 1.35-1.67. The horizon extension was worth testing - the
reasoning behind it was sound and its per-trade edge is genuinely larger - but
it trades too rarely to convert that edge into monthly return, and adding it
to the incumbent dilutes rather than improves.

3-6R a month at a 10R drawdown is not a strategy that has not been found yet.
It is a MAR that does not appear in the documented record.
