===== FOUR MODELS x SEVEN MARKETS, EACH JUDGED ALONE =====

Two questions, answered separately. First: do the existing models have an edge
on any market other than NQ, standing on their own rather than as portfolio
legs? Second: does Dalton's open-type classification - the "Type Open" material
in TO_IB/iborrotd.mp4 - produce a third strategy?

Data: Dukascopy M1, 2023-01..2026-08, roughly 900 sessions per market. Each
market is run at TWO cost levels, because on most of them the gap decides the
answer:

===== 4. THE DALTON OPEN TYPES: THE CLAIM DOES NOT REPLICATE =====

Mechanical definitions on closed 5-minute bars, thresholds as fractions of the
prior 14-day range so they cannot become date filters:
  OD   drives from the open and never trades back through it
  OTD  a shallow probe against the eventual direction, then a drive
  ORR  a real excursion one way, rejected, then a drive back through the open
  OA   no directional conviction
Entry at the close of the 30-minute window, stop at the window's adverse
extreme, target fixed, forced exit at the session close.

The source claims 76.4% wins at 1:1 on 85 trades. Measured, at 1R:

  market      n      WR     PF     expR   CI lo
  NQ        252   55.6%   1.22   +0.087  -0.027
  ES        172   54.1%   1.10   +0.046  -0.098
  RTY       327   50.8%   0.97   -0.012  -0.110
  YM        261   49.4%   0.93   -0.033  -0.145
  DAX        92   48.9%   0.92   -0.036  -0.232
  SX5E      134   44.0%   0.59   -0.239  -0.398
  FTSE      198   47.0%   0.83   -0.084  -0.217

The best win rate on any market is 55.6%, twenty points below the claim, and not
one market clears zero. Three further findings kill the classification outright.

FIRST - ORR, the type the whole narrative is built on, barely exists. Under a
mechanical definition it appears on 21 of 906 NQ sessions, 4 on DAX, 5 on SX5E.
Whatever the source is identifying by eye, it is not a rule.

SECOND - the type you are told to SKIP performs as well as the types you are
told to trade. NQ at a 2R target:

  what                    n      WR     PF     expR   CI lo   DD_R
  source pick OTD+ORR   252   48.4%   1.28   +0.130  -0.017    7.5
  OTD only              231   48.9%   1.36   +0.163  +0.007    6.6
  OA only = SKIP list   369   43.4%   1.15   +0.085  -0.049   13.8
  every session         896   45.1%   1.18   +0.092  +0.012   11.6

THIRD - and this is decisive - deleting the classification entirely and trading
EVERY session loses nothing. The null model reads +0.092R with a lower bound of
+0.012 on 896 trades; the source's pick reads +0.130R on 252 with a lower bound
BELOW zero. The best classified cell, OTD alone, is +0.163R, and the gap over
the null is 0.8 sigma - noise, out of a grid of 126 cells where roughly six
false positives are expected by chance.

Verdict: the open-type classification carries no measurable information. What
the grid actually found is unrelated to Dalton, and it is the null model.

===== WHAT NOT TO BELIEVE YET =====
1. THE COST LEVEL DECIDES IT. Every number above is at futures cost. At prop
   CFD cost the drive model falls to +0.062R with a lower bound of -0.017 - it
   does NOT clear zero on a CFD account. This strategy needs exchange-level
   costs, or a broker whose NQ spread is near a tick. That is the single most
   important caveat in this file.
2. IT IS ONE DAY OLD. The retest model took weeks of null models, holdouts and
   two retracted claims before it was trusted. The drive model has had one
   afternoon. The checks it passed are the right ones and it passed them
   cleanly, but the retest model also looked clean before the FTMO cross-check
   inverted min_break_or_frac.
3. THE TARGET WAS CHOSEN AT THE EDGE. Expectancy rises monotonically from 1R to
   3R with no interior peak, which means the target sits at the boundary of what
   was searched rather than on a plateau. Monotone target curves usually mean
   the edge lives in the tail of the distribution. trim20 survives, so the tail
   is not the whole story, but it halves the edge.
4. 2023 IS NEGATIVE (-0.025R). Three of four years positive, not four.
5. THE THREE-LEG MAR OF 4.45 IS MEASURED ON THE DATA THAT SELECTED THE LEGS.
   Treat +3.71 R/mo as the optimistic end of a range, not a forecast.
