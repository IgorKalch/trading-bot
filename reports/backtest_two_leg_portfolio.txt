===== THE FOUR PRIORITY STEPS, AND A TWO-LEG PORTFOLIO =====

--- STEP 1+2: A CHEAPER EXPRESSION OF THE CONFLUENCE VIEW ---
Tried entering on the IB break instead of at 10:30, with stops at the IB
midpoint, a recent swing, and a fraction of the IB, and targets at fixed RR and
at the 100%/50% measured move. NQ, 905 sessions, futures-tick costs.

  entry     stop   target     n     WR    PF    expR   CI lo   DD_R
  open1030  ibfar  rr1.5    744  50.4%  1.17  +0.066  -0.002   12.6
  break     ibfar  rr1.5    616  52.8%  1.26  +0.076  +0.013   13.7
  break     ibfar  mm50     616  62.3%  1.17  +0.041  -0.005    6.2
  break     swing  rr1.5    616  48.9%  1.16  +0.065  -0.014   14.2

The break entry clears zero where the 10:30 entry does not, and it looks like
the answer. It is not, and the null model says why:

  10:30 entry:  real +0.066R  vs null -0.108R  ->  +4.7 sd
  break entry:  real +0.076R  vs null +0.056R  ->  +0.7 sd

A random side entered on ITS OWN break of the IB makes +0.056R. Waiting for the
break means the break has already told you the direction, so the confluence
filter has almost nothing left to add. The information is real at 10:30 and
spent by 11:00.

That is the answer to steps 1 and 2: there is no cheaper expression. The signal
predicts the day's direction, not its path, so it needs a stop wide enough to
survive the path - which is the whole Initial Balance.

--- STEP 3: THE C-PERIOD RULE, AND IT HAS NO LOOKAHEAD ---
Signal: the 10:55-11:00 close outside the IB. Variant A scans from 10:30 (as
published), variant B only from 11:00 (tradable).

  market  signal   fires        uncond.   variant A   variant B   lookahead
  NQ      sig_up   159 (17.5%)   12.5%      28.3%       28.3%       0.0pp
  NQ      sig_dn   145 (16.0%)   15.1%      44.1%       44.1%       0.0pp
  DAX     sig_up   185 (19.7%)   25.9%      49.2%       49.2%       0.0pp
  DAX     sig_dn   138 (14.7%)   24.7%      61.6%       61.6%       0.0pp

The firing rate matches the published 17.8% / 15.0% on NQ almost exactly, and
the lift of 2.27x to 2.92x brackets the published 2.59x / 2.62x. A and B are
identical to the decimal, because a 100% extension needs a full IB range beyond
the boundary and the signal bar closing just outside never reaches that far.
So the published figure was clean - a rare thing in this material.

--- STEP 4: THE TWO LEGS ARE UNCORRELATED, AND THAT IS THE RESULT ---
Monthly correlation between the retest model and the confluence trade: +0.02.
Daily: -0.04. Structural, not fitted - different hour (09:30-10:00 against
10:30), different trigger, different instrument behaviour.

                              R/month    DD      annual MAR   +months
  retest alone                +1.155    8.32R      1.67         61%
  confluence alone            +1.112    8.65R      1.54         61%
  COMBINED, half risk each    +1.134    5.30R      2.57         64%

  in-sample  (26 mo)          +1.148    5.30R      2.60         62%
  out-of-sample (18 mo)       +1.113    2.37R      5.63         67%

  by year: 2023 +0.872  2024 +1.522  2025 +1.008  2026 +1.133 R/month

Same monthly return as either leg alone, at 64% of the drawdown. In-sample and
out-of-sample agree to within 0.035R a month. Every year positive.

The drawdown reduction is not luck: averaging two uncorrelated series of similar
volatility should cut the standard deviation by root two, and 8.5 / 1.41 = 6.0
against the 5.30 measured.

--- IN MONEY ---
  risk 1.0% per 1R of the combined series:  ~13.6%/yr,  5.3% max drawdown
  risk 1.5%:                                ~20.4%/yr,  8.0% max drawdown
  retest alone at 1.0%:                     ~13.9%/yr,  8.3% max drawdown

The combination buys the same return at two thirds of the drawdown, or half
again the return at the same drawdown. Against a 10% account limit that is the
difference between an uncomfortable 1% and a comfortable 1.5%.

--- WHAT IS STILL WRONG WITH IT ---
1. The confluence leg's per-trade expectancy is +0.066R with a lower bound of
   -0.002. It does not individually clear zero. What clears is its direction
   signal, at 4.7 sd above a same-days random-side null, and the portfolio
   benefit comes from the correlation rather than from that leg's size.
2. One instrument. On DAX the same trade is +0.021R and the cheap expression is
   worthless (-0.5 sd against its null).
3. Still NDXUSD, a CFD proxy for NQ, and slippage is still assumed at one tick.
4. This is the first combination in the project that helped, and the reason it
   helped is exactly the reason the earlier ones did not: both legs have an edge.
