===== CAN THIS PASS A PROP CHALLENGE IN ONE MONTH? =====

Measured: expectancy +0.146R, 7.9 trades a month, historical max drawdown 10.3R.

--- A. THE ARITHMETIC ---
  expected profit per month = 7.9 x 0.146 = 1.16R
  historical worst drawdown = 10.3R
  the drawdown is NINE MONTHS of expected profit

  To make 10% in one month you must risk 8.6% per trade.
  At that risk the historical drawdown becomes 89% of the account, and a
  seven-trade losing streak - which happened - costs 60%.

  The condition for a one-month pass is: expected monthly R > max drawdown R.
  We have 1.16R against 10.3R. Short by a factor of 8.9.

  Closing that gap needs one of: 71 trades a month instead of 8, or +1.31R
  per trade instead of +0.146R, or a 1.1R maximum drawdown instead of 10.3R.

--- B. MORE INSTRUMENTS WAS THE ONLY REAL LEVER. IT DOES NOT EXIST. ---

Ordered by cost/R the column is almost monotone. NQ is the only market on
earth-and-Dukascopy where this model has an edge, and the reason is that it
swings 36 points on the setup while its tick is 0.25. Trading four markets
at once would have quadrupled monthly R against a drawdown growing more
slowly - that was the one honest route to a faster pass, and there is only
one instrument to trade.

--- C. WHAT A ONE-MONTH ATTEMPT ACTUALLY LOOKS LIKE ---
20,000 bootstrap runs per cell, drawn from the real 349-trade distribution.
Pass = reach +10% before a 10% drawdown.

  risk/trade      1 month        2 months       3 months       6 months      12 months
                pass blown     pass blown     pass blown     pass blown     pass blown
       1%         0%    0%       5%    0%      16%    1%      45%    4%      77%    9%
       2%        14%    2%      39%   11%      52%   18%      70%   26%      72%   28%
       3%        30%   14%      53%   28%      62%   33%      65%   35%      65%   35%
       5%        50%   36%      58%   42%      58%   42%      58%   42%      58%   42%
       8%        57%   42%      57%   43%      57%   43%      57%   43%      57%   43%
      10%        57%   43%      57%   43%      57%   43%      57%   43%      57%   43%

The pass rate SATURATES at 57%. Past about 8% risk, more leverage buys
nothing: you either hit the target in a few trades or you are gone. A coin
flip would give roughly 50/50 here; the entire measured edge is worth seven
percentage points on that bet.

And it is worse than the table shows. Prop accounts also carry a ~5% DAILY
loss limit. At 5% risk per trade one loss ends the day at the limit; at 8%
one loss fails the account outright. The high-risk column is not reachable in
practice - the realistic ceiling is about 2% per trade, which is a 14% chance
of passing in a month and a 2% chance of blowing up.

--- D. THE REAL CHOICES ---
  0.50% risk:  0% in 1 month, 37% in 12, 81% in 24, essentially never blows up
  0.75% risk:  0% in 1 month, 64% in 12, 90% in 24, 5% blow-up over 24 months
  1.00% risk:  0% in 1 month, 77% in 12, 88% in 24, 11% blow-up
  2.00% risk: 14% in 1 month, 72% in 12, but 28% blow-up over 12 months

There is no configuration of this strategy that passes a 10% challenge in one
month with better than coin-flip odds, and none that does it at a risk level
a daily loss limit permits. That is not a tuning problem. An edge of +0.146R
on eight trades a month makes about 1.2R a month; a 10% target at a survivable
risk is 13 to 20R of profit. The gap is arithmetic.
